Wash your hands then moisten a cotton pad with nail polish remover and sweep over your nails to remove any oils and impurities.

Next file and buff your nails to smooth any uneven areas and achieve your desired length, then apply one coat of Townhouse Base Coat - to help strengthen the nail.

Apply one thin coat of your chosen Townhouse Flawless Polish, using our easy to use brush to flawlessly coat your nail, working from base to tip. Repeat with another layer or two, to achieve your desired colour finish. Finish with a Townhouse Top Coat allowing to dry fully, before adding a drop of Townhouse nourishing cuticle oil to the base of each nail bed.
