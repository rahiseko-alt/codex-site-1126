Rive Careers

build the future of interactive design with us

We’re rethinking how designers, animators, and developers work together — making that new way of building software accessible to everyone. Our customers are chart-topping mobile apps, AAA game studios, Fortune 500 vehicle companies, and more. Rive gives these companies the tools to build award-winning interactive experiences across apps, games, websites, products, and vehicles.

The Rive Team

Who we are

Rive is a low-ego team of designers, developers, and dreamers who care deeply about craft. We’re scrappy, curious, kind, and candid. We help each other, share credit, and ship fast.


We’re global and mostly remote, united by the mission to build the best tool in the world for designers, animators, and developers — a place where each can operate at full power, together.

See us in action

You’ll collaborate with people who’ve shaped the tools you already use, like the creator of Lottie, and veterans of top creative and game studios.
