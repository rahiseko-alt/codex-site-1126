Eco Pulse

2026 ::: Health

An e-commerce website for Eco Pulse — a smart hydration product that makes drinking enough water easier.

:: Role

Product Design / Digital Design

:: Team

Zajno — 3D

:: Timeline

4 weeks

:: Services

E-commerce design / 3D

:: Platform

Website, desktop & mobile

(01)

Eco Pulse is a smart hydration product that helps people build a simple daily habit: drinking enough water.

(02)

The store had to sell more than a bottle — it had to explain the technology quickly and make it feel like a natural part of everyday life.

:: / FINDINGS

The product explains itself

Features are explained through the product in motion rather than long blocks of text, so the page stays light and easy to follow.

:: / APPROACHFrom first look to checkout.

An e-commerce experience that feels as clean and easy as the product, guiding visitors from first look to purchase without friction.
