(01)

BRIK is a brain-training platform with short interactive exercises that train reaction speed, focus and mental agility.

(02)

The main task was motivation: making daily training feel rewarding and quick, so people actually come back tomorrow.

:: / APPROACHShort, clear, rewarding.

01 —

User flows

02 —

03 —

Every session short, with progress always visible.

04 —

UI design

Instant feedback and micro-interactions after each exercise.

Focus first

Bold shapes, bright accents and playful micro-interactions keep training engaging, while the structure stays calm enough to concentrate.

A habit is built by the experience, not by reminders.
