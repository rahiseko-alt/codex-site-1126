A website for Evergreen Theragnostics — a company that develops and produces radiopharmaceuticals used to find and treat cancer.

:: Role

:: Team

:: Timeline

:: Services

Web design / 3D direction

Website, desktop & mobile

(01)

Evergreen Theragnostics is a privately funded company working in nuclear medicine. Its products help doctors both see cancer and treat it.

(02)

The challenge: explain a complex scientific process clearly, without losing either accuracy or a sense of warmth.

:: / APPROACHHow we got there.

01 —

Usermap

Who comes to the site, what each of them needs to understand first, and in which order.

02 —

Direction

Calm structure and strong contrast, with motion reserved for moments that explain something.

03 —

Wireframes

One idea per section, so the science reads as a sequence rather than a wall of facts.

04 —

Design

Typography, colour and 3D scenes brought into one quiet, confident system.

:: / FINDINGS

Visual Experience

Science sites often feel either cold or overly decorative. We aimed for the middle: cinematic motion where it explains something, and quiet layouts everywhere else, so the content always comes first.

Motion only where it explains something. Quiet everywhere else.

— design principle

:: / DETAILSSmall things that carry the idea.

:: / OUTCOMEWhat changed. What comes next.

Final Result

A website that makes a highly specialised field understandable at a glance and presents Evergreen as a confident, forward-looking company in nuclear medicine.
