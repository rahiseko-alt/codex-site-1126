Pulsar

2025 ::: Data

An interactive data story about how artificial intelligence has evolved — and how fast.

:: Role

Digital Design

:: Team

Client team

:: Timeline

6 weeks

:: Services

Data storytelling / interaction design

:: Platform

Website, desktop

(01)

Pulsar is an interactive website that tells the story of AI progress through data, narrative and small game-like moments.

(02)

The goal was to turn large, abstract datasets into something people want to explore — not just read.

:: / FINDINGS

Explore, don’t skim

Each chart is built as an interaction. Visitors scrub, compare and discover, so the numbers stay memorable.

:: / APPROACHFrom data to story.

01 —

Data research

Which numbers really show the growth of AI — and which only look impressive.

02 —

Storyline

A clear beginning, middle and end, explored at the user’s own pace.

03 —

Wireframes

Every chart planned as an interaction, not an illustration.

04 —

Interaction design

Scrubbing, comparing and small games that make the numbers stick.

If people can play with the data, they remember it.

— design principle

:: / DETAILSSmall things that carry the idea.

:: / OUTCOMEWhat changed. What comes next.

Final Result

An engaging data experience that makes the growth of AI intuitive and invites people to explore instead of skim.
