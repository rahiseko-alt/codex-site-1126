(01)

Smart Energy is a dashboard concept that helps people see and manage how their home uses energy, as it happens.

(02)

The challenge was to make technical energy data feel friendly and actionable for everyday users.

:: / APPROACHNumbers connected to real rooms.

01 —

Research

What people actually want to know about their energy use.

02 —

Direction

A clean dashboard grid paired with a 3D view of the home.

03 —

:: / FINDINGS

Context makes it clear

A clean dashboard grid with simple visualisations makes consumption easy to read, while the 3D home adds context and a sense of control.

Data feels friendly when it lives in a familiar place.

— design principle
