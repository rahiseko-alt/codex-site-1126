Eden’s Veil Case is great!

Purchased the Eden Veil Case in Grimthorn XL for my (first) PC Build! It arrived quickly and in great condition. I was surprised that the Case’s Base was that of a different manufacturer/Designer: Fractal’s North Design XL. However, I’m not mad about it. It meant it was easier to find a guide online from somebody building with the case. Plus, the detailing is still just absolutely gorgeous. Some details from the OG case you miss in the pictures: gold/brass buttons and plugs along the top of the case that matches the wrapping on the feet, the spot for the power supply has a dust tray that can be easily removed for cleaning, and the metal side of the case has an extra plate with rubber grommets to hide the wiring behind! The XL is definitely bigger than I need, but I eventually want to switch to water cooling and it provides slots for the tubing in the top brackets. The design added by Ironside is pristine and beautiful; there were no scratches found. They kept the plastic film on the other side of the glass. I would’ve loved if they reapplied the film for the design side to give it extra smudge protection, especially as you take out the panel to build the PC. However, the packaging used was plenty to keep it safe during transit and generally unboxing! TL;DR despite my nitpicks, this is a *really* high quality case. I’m a longtime looker, first time purchaser and I’m glad I made the purchase.
