About

In a realm between realms, beyond the reach of mortal maps lies Eden’s Veil, a sanctuary of knowledge that reveals itself only to those chosen.

Eden's Veil

/EDEN'S
VEIL

Beyond the Ordinary

Crafted to resonate with your unique taste and aesthetic, Eden’s Veil is a magical blend of design and function that takes a step outside of conventional.

The Sacred Sigil

Hand-painted sigils grace the front panel, masterfully crafted to evoke a sense of time-worn elegance with an artfully distressed, aged finish.

Wondrously Wooden

The front panels are crafted from real oak or walnut, featuring slatted designs that bring a touch of natural elegance to your setup. These carefully selected materials add warmth and texture, blending organic beauty with modern functionality.

Widowbloom

They say there is a garden beyond Eden’s Veil that no gardener will tend. The flowers there open at dusk, petals dark as a widow’s dress, and turn only to the shadowed moon. Deep violet glass, tangled branches, and blood red roses beneath a crescent moon bring the forgotten garden of Widowbloom back to life. Widowbloom is our newest stained glass design for Eden’s Veil, printed pane by pane. The garden is open.

The Dawn/Dusk Dichotomy

Available in two captivating colorways—Frostvine, evoking the brighter and more vibrant energy of daylight, and Ravenroot, reflecting the more subdued and moody hues a moonlit evening.

Grimthorn Emerges: A Haunting Reimagining of Eden’s Veil

Meet Grimthorn, the darker evolution of Eden’s Veil. Inspired by toxic plants and shadowy gardens, this edition wraps the design in deep purples and magentas, perfect for those drawn to the beauty of the macabre.
