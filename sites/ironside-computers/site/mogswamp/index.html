Terminal 1: Mogswamp Edition

Mogswamp Edition

A Mogswamp twist on Terminal 1, a retro-futuristic reimagining of the personal gaming computer. Every panel is UV-printed directly onto the chassis: a permanent, precise finish giving the control panel its crisp details and the body a seamless, factory-grade feel that won’t peel, fade, or lift over time.

About

A machine that bridges eras and paints a picture of a future that never came. Boxy and ultra-minimal in form, softened by rounded windows inspired by midcentury industrial design. 

Terminal 1: Mogswamp Edition

/Mogswamp

Mogswamp Special Edition

Developed in collaboration with Mogswamp, this edition reimagines Terminal 1 in cool bluish-green tones that leans into a more a playful, retro-console inspired aesthetic.

The Future Reimagined

The future imagined through the lens of the past. Every detail nods to the golden age of tech design and early computing, from the iconic green terminal glow to the boxy silhouettes and restrained, minimal palettes of early machines.

Green Screen Revival

Paying homage to the monochrome monitors that started it all, Terminal 1 features a printed green control panel detailed with tiny text and interface glyphs with playful references tucked beneath its technical veneer for those who still read the fine print.

UV Printed Precision

Every panel on Terminal 1 is UV-printed directly onto the chassis. The result is a seamless, permanent finish giving the control panel its crisp precision and the body a seamless, factory-grade feel that won’t peel, fade, or lift over time.
