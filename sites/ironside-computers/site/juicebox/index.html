Juicebox

/juicebox

Gaming Needs More Purple!

It’s a comment we kept seeing across the internet, and we completely agree. One of our favorite things to do is create designs people truly love, even if it’s not something you normally see in the gaming space. So we made Grape Juicebox.

Limited Drop: Peaches & Cream

Blending warm peach tones with creamy pink highlights, Peaches & Cream is the sweetest ever addition to the Ironside Juicebox shelf. Getting the dreamy colors exactly right took a lot of time, but was totally worth it. You’ll understand when it arrives - it’s hard to take your eyes off of these delightful pastels. Peaches & Cream is a limited drop — once it’s gone, it’s gone.

Juiced Up

Now built on a full ATX chassis, Juicebox gives you more room to play. Whether you’re running high-end GPUs, custom cooling, or future-proofing your build, the expanded layout offers the flexibility and airflow to support it all without losing the compact charm that made Juicebox a favorite.

Freshly Squeezed

Each Juicebox variant has been illustrated in-house in irresistible flavors like honeydew melon and peach, and we obsessed – hard – over making the colors of each design utterly delightful. The chassis features a mix of glass and metal panels, all UV-printed for vibrant, durable, and highly detailed designs.

The Juicy Bits

From the all new magnetic straw that snaps satisfyingly into place to the playful nutrition label printed on the back, Juicebox is filled with thoughtful details that bring the design to life.

Depth in Every Drop ✦₊⁺⟡

A new glass front panel brings added depth to the design. Subtle shadows shift beneath the surface as the light changes, which highlights the chassis’ build materials in a unique, elevated way.

“In recent years, it feels like the playful spirit of all things ‘kawaii’ has spread like wildfire, and it brings us so much joy to bring some of that energy into something as traditionally serious as a gaming computer. Each of our Juiceboxes has been illustrated in house in delightful variations such as honeydew melon green and peach pink. The Juicebox line is undeniably unique and has brought many gleeful smiles to our team. I have no doubt that they will continue to delight their owners for years to come.”

Domenic Apice

Ironside Creative Director
