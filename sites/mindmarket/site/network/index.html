Questions buyers ask about multi-country research

Which countries does MindMarket cover?

MindMarket runs qualitative research in 60+ countries across the Americas, Europe, the Middle East, Africa, Asia-Pacific and East Asia, with moderators who live in each market. Your market is not on the list? Tell us where: we run studies in most countries and confirm what is possible within 12 business hours.

How does a multi-country qualitative study work?

One brief, one project manager and the same method in every market. You brief us once, and your project manager in London, Dubai or Mexico shapes the study with you. We recruit locally in each country and adapt the discussion guide to each culture. Local moderators run the sessions in the participants' language, with live viewing for your team. We analyse every market with one framework and deliver one report that compares them. More in our guide to international qualitative research.

Do your moderators speak the local language?

Yes: every MindMarket session is moderated in the participants' own language by a moderator who lives in the market, so people answer the way they talk at home. We translate what your team needs, from transcripts to the final report.

How long does a multi-country study take?

A typical multi-country project takes 8 to 12 weeks from planning to final report. Recruitment is the biggest variable, because some audiences take longer to find in some markets. For a fast first read in one market, First Listen delivers in 2 to 3 weeks.

How much does a multi-country study cost?

MindMarket prices multi-country qualitative research per market: full-service studies start at $40,000 for the first market, and each additional market adds from $8,000, because the design, the discussion guide and the analysis are shared. Recruitment, local-language moderation and translation repeat in each market. Estimate your study in 30 seconds on our pricing page; your itemised quote follows within 48 hours of a first call.

Will I deal with one contact for all markets?

Yes: one MindMarket project manager runs your whole study, whatever the number of markets, from the brief to the debrief. Our project managers work from London, Dubai and Mexico, so one of them shares your working hours.
