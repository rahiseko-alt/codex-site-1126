Media Hub

All your team’s footage, where you create.

Media Hub is the shared media library inside Slate. Upload once, organize by folder and campaign, search by term, and pull photos and video straight into any project on web or in the Slate mobile app (iOS and Android). Connected to Getty Images, PhotoShelter, Greenfly, Slack, and Sprout Social, with AI search across everything you’ve shot coming soon.

Collaboration

Review where you create. Replace the round-trip.

Slate’s collaboration tools keep the whole team, and anyone outside it, in one shared project: cloud-saved work, external review links, comments pinned to the exact frame, @mentions, project statuses, Editor and Viewer access, and approvals from the Slate mobile app. The post gets better without the file ever leaving Slate, and without a second tool to review it in.
