    From Concept to Creation:

    Our Custom press on service is an elevated process that blends the highest quality, art and your unique style. Here's how it works:

    01

    In-Studio Consultation:

    Book a 30-minute consultation to measure your nails and choose your shape, length, and design – either from our Lookbook or inspired by your own vision.

    02

    Design Creation:

    Your nail artist will send a preview of your custom press ons during the creation process to make sure it’s coming to life exactly how you imagined.

    03

    Application or Pick Up:

    Your custom press ons will be ready in 1–2 weeks. You can choose to pick them up or book an application appointment.
