    What’s included?

    Single Color

    Our signature Paintbox dry manicure includes prep, shaping, and single color application in the color of your choice all within a 45 min appointment. Our artists consult with you to make sure the color best suits your event, skin tone, and lifestyle.

    *Please note we can NOT remove acrylic, hard gel, sculpting gel, or dip powder manicures. We offer a gentle removal process by hand and only remove soft gels with proper booking of this additional service.

    Classic Design

    Choose a design from the Classic options in our seasonal Lookbook. This 60 min appointment includes prep, shaping, and a personal consultation with your Artist.

    *Please note we can NOT remove acrylic, hard gel, sculpting gel, or dip powder manicures. We offer a gentle removal process by hand and only remove soft gels with proper booking of this additional service.

    Luxe Design

    Choose a design from the Luxe options in our seasonal Lookbook. This 75 min appointment includes prep, shaping, and a personal consultation with your Artist.

    *Please note we can NOT remove acrylic, hard gel, sculpting gel, or dip powder manicures. We offer a gentle removal process by hand and only remove soft gels with proper booking of this additional service.

    Healthy Nail Extensions

    Natural nails are trimmed and filed and cuticles are prepared with a dry manicure. Choose from our selection of round, almond, and square shapes in Aprés short or medium lengths. Extensions are then applied and bonded with gel and finished with an application of the gel polish color or design of your choice.

    *Please note we can NOT remove acrylic, hard gel, sculpting gel, or dip powder manicures. We offer a gentle removal process by hand and only remove soft gels with proper booking of this additional service.

    Deluxe Session

    Enjoy extra time with your Artist in our two hour Deluxe Session. Choose any Classic or Luxe design from our seasonal Lookbook and customize your color palette, add foil or chrome, etc. The Deluxe Session is perfect for those with extra long nails or small group mani dates. Gel removal is included.

    *Please note we can NOT remove acrylic, hard gel, sculpting gel, or dip powder manicures. We offer a gentle removal process by hand and only remove soft gels with proper booking of this additional service.

    Bridal Manicure

    This appointment is 1 hour and 45 minutes to give you extra time for a consultation with one of our top artists so that we can really match your nails to the color scheme, vibe, dress, and flowers of your wedding. Choose your design from our curated Bridal or Seasonal Lookbook. Includes gel removal.

    *Please note we can NOT remove acrylic, hard gel, sculpting gel, or dip powder manicures. We offer a gentle removal process by hand and only remove soft gels with proper booking of this additional service.

    Nail Rehab

    IBX is a strengthening treatment that repairs, toughens, and protects the natural nails, ideal for thinning or splitting nails. Nails are shaped, buffed, and cleansed before application.

    Hand Ritual Massage

    Sit back, relax, and enjoy a 10-minute Hand Ritual Massage to finish out your manicure and elevate your self-care game. Using ultra-hydrating products and targeting pressure points in the hands, the Hand Ritual Massage will leave you feeling relaxed and rejuvenated.
