      How to Apply

      Step 1: Prep

      File and shape your natural nails with the pink file, push your cuticles back with the wood stick, and cleanse with the alcohol prep pad. Proper prep is the key to longer lasting press ons!

      Step 2: Size

      Find your best fit and choose your sizes. If between two sizes, go with the bigger size. Pro tip: once you've found the right sizes, note the numbers on the underside of each tip for next time.

      Step 3: Apply

      Using the gel tabs, brush-on glue, or a combination of both, apply the press ons one finger at a time. Avoid getting hands wet directly after applying to allow the glue to fully dry. For the full tutorial, click here.
