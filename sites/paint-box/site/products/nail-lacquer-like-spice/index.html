    A cinnamon brick with undertones of both butterscotch and ginger, this gorgeous shade gives your nails an immediate feel-good factor, as well as serious sophistication.

    With its rounded, flexible bristles, our unique brush is able to pick up just the right amount of lacquer for maximum efficiency, plus zero streaking.

    The rich, terracotta shade (with both ginger and caramel undertones) is an easy way to elevate your style.

    How To

    Step 1

    A shiny, reflective topcoat transforms the terracotta tone into a deeper, luxe-looking caramel.

    Step 2

    Want a little more edge to your nails? A matte topcoat atop Like Spice gives them a sexy, hazy texture.

    Step 3

    Give new meaning to your manicure: Like Spice + Like Spring is an on-trend take on retro staples.
