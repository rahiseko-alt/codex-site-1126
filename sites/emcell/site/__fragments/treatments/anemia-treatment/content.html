Anemia Fetal Stem Cell Treatment at EmCell

Anemia is a disease related to a decrease in the amount of red blood cells (erythrocytes) or hemoglobin in the blood. This can cause a decline in the level of oxygen carried from the lungs to other tissues.

Anemia is the most common blood disorder, affecting about a third of the global population. There are many causes of anemia and many methods of treatment.

EmCell offers a highly effective fetal stem cell treatment for anemia that can addresses and positively impact the pathogenetic component of the disease.

About treatment

Fetal stem cell therapy proves to be effective in the treatment of primary and secondary anemia, including aplastic and hereditary anemias.

Many patients who have not responded to standard treatment methods have sought our Anemia treatment program.

While the purpose of standard pharmacological therapy is mainly to stimulate the patient’s damaged bone marrow, fetal stem cell therapy focuses on applying fetal stem cells of erythrocyte precursors, which increases the pool of the cells responsible for hematopoiesis (blood creation) in the patient’s body.

Fetal stem cell therapy for patients with sickle cell anemia helps to reduce the frequency of crises, as well as decrease the need for repeated transfusions of packed red blood cells.

In the cases of hereditary anemias, fetal stem cell therapy is also beneficial.

All patients who have undergone our anemia treatment program have seen measurable laboratory result improvements and overall improvements. Treatment results can vary depending on the underlying cause of anemia.
