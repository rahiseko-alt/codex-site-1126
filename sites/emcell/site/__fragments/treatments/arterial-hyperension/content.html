Fetal Stem Cell Treatment for Arterial Hypertension at EmCell

Arterial hypertension, also known as high blood pressure, is a chronic disease related to elevation of blood pressure inside the arteries

According to the world’s medical statistics, arterial hypertension is one of the main causes of death worldwide. The main symptom of a hypertension condition is difficulty breathing during physical activity. This generally starts slowly, getting worse over time. Other symptoms include fatigue, chest pain and faintness.

Patients with arterial hypertension also often suffer from swelling in ankles and legs.

About treatment

EmCell offers fetal stem cell therapy in a combined treatment course, the purpose of which is both to improve the condition of arterial walls and blood pressure stabilization.

Fetal stem cell treatment for arterial hypertension is based on the administration of healthy fetal stem cells into damaged organs – blood vessels and surrounding tissues. This procedure contributes to blood vessel thickening and restoring the proper blood flow throughout the whole body.

Fetal stem cells administered into recipient’s body enter the cardiac muscle and produce various biologically active substances, such as growth factors and cytokines. This results in improved heart functioning. Under the influence of the microenvironment, fetal stem cells develop into a phenotype of cardiomyocytes. 

Also part of our Arterial Hypertension protocol is the application of pre-neuronal stem cells that normalize nervous vascular control resulting in overall crisis prevention. That is mainly how fetal stem cell therapy can be used to restore the functional properties of the heart muscle. 

Within 3–5 months after fetal stem cell therapy, 75% of patients demonstrate stable decline in blood pressure and reduction of hypotensive therapy by approximately 50%.

All patients note positive treatment effects such as disappearance of headache, stabilization and normalization of blood pressure. They have also experienced improvements in sleep, mental ability and libido (both in men and women), and a general return of vivacity. All signs of new rejuvenation and regeneration.
