Fetal Stem Cell Treatment for Muscular Dystrophy (MD) at EmCell

Muscular dystrophies are a group of genetic diseases that affect skeletal muscles and often heart muscle.

Muscular Dystrophy (MD) is caused by mutations in genes that code for specific muscle proteins. The symptoms of MD include muscle weakness and progressive muscle wasting.

About treatment

EmCell offers fetal stem cell therapy for different types of muscular dystrophy.

Fetal stem cell therapy has proven to be among most promising methods of MD treatment, which has lead to the reduction of muscle wasting and slowing down of the manifestation of primary symptoms.

Using fetal stem cells, we strive to reach two main goals:

  • Production of healthy muscle fibers: We inject fetal stem cells without any genetic defect that causes MD, and they are delivered to the patient’s muscles, where they work to replace damaged muscle fiber cells. 
  • Reduced inflammation: Damaged muscles become quite inflamed and this inflammation speeds up muscle degeneration. Fetal stem cells release chemicals that reduce inflammation, slowing the progress of the disease, and increase regeneration.
EmCell’s team of doctors has extensive experience treating Muscular Dystrophy (MD).

We are ready to perform stem cell treatment for different stages of MD, at different ages of patients and at different levels of muscle atrophy with the average improvements rate of 88%.

EmCell continues to be excited about its progress and track record in treating Muscular Dystrophy.
