About treatment

During our life, the self-renewing capacities of our body naturally decline. This happens because the amount of the stem cells in our body gradually decrease, and the ability of our stem cells to multiply and replace aging cells with new ones diminishes over time.

Stress, junk food, bad habits, poor environment, and inactive lifestyle also contribute to make our body and mind age more rapidly.

When performing our fetal stem cells treatment for our patients, we: (1) increase the amount of new active stem cells, and (2) stimulate patient’s own stem cells in the body to further self-rejuvenation. We help our patients to become younger in a natural, biological way.

EmCell’s Anti-Aging treatment course can also include additional treatment options, such as Hyperbaric oxygenation (HBO), facial treatment with stem cells, massages, and Halo therapy (salt room), which can be combined together with fetal stem cell treatment.

Each of these options can be recommended by our doctors, and chosen by the patient, and added to the treatment program.

This procedure is safe, painless and there are no adverse reactions or side effects.

When fetal stem cells are introduced into a body, they expand stem cell reserves and trigger a self-renewal process in all the body’s organs and tissues. They also stimulate the body’s own dormant stem cells, which improves the functioning of the internal organs, boosts the immune system, and increases energy levels. The effects observed in our patients have shown rejuvenation throughout the entire body.

All of our Anti-Aging patients have reported varying degrees of positive improvements. The level of these improvements depends on the patient’s condition: such as any health problems before the treatment, and lifestyle after the treatment.
