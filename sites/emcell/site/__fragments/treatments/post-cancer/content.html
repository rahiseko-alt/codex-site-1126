Accelerate Recovery After Cancer with Stem Cell Therapy

Improvements in Post Cancer patients that can be expected after stem cell therapy

During the first month after stem cell treatment, patients may observe the following effects:

  • Improved overall condition and appetite
  • Diminished weakness and sweating
  • Increased capacity to work and engage in self-care
  • Better emotional status, cognitive abilities and memory

Within 1-2 months after fetal stem cells treatment the next effects can be observed as well:

  • Improved haemopoesis
  • Improved immunological profile
  • Reduced levels of inflammatory markers
  • Higher quality of life

Fetal Stem Cell Treatment for Post Cancer patients at EmCell

Fetal stem cell treatment is not an alternative to standard protocols of treating cancer. Stem cell therapy is beneficial only after surgery and/or traditional chemo- and/or radiotherapy, and only after a full remission has been acquired.

Fetal stem cell therapy contributes to formation of anti-tumor immunity, which is crucial for preventing relapse and new metastatic disease.

About the treatment

Fetal stem cell treatment can be done for patients with stabilized condition proved by results of instrumental and laboratory findings – there should be no primary tumor growth or new metastasis formation to be accepted for fetal stem cell therapy at EmCell.

As conventional cancer treatment has many side effects, fetal stem cell treatment is aimed mainly at:

  • Partial restoration of anti-tumor immunity and haemopoesis
  • Improvement in patient’s quality of life – reduced weakness, better overall condition, both physical and psycho-emotional
Fetal stem cell therapy contributes to the formation of anti-tumor immunity, which is crucial for preventing cancer’s relapse. This treatment gives a chance for the body to restore the damaged functions from traditional cancer therapies in order get life back to a more normal state.
