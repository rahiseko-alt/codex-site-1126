Stem Cell Therapy for Diabetes

Expected improvements in Diabetes patients after fetal stem cell therapy

In combination with traditional treatment of Type 1 diabetes, fetal stem cells (FSC) can help:

  • Decrease the auto-aggression of immune cells against β-cells
  • Prevent the destruction of functional β-cells
  • Restoration of patient’s own β-cells and improve their functional capacity
  • Prevent diabetes-related complications that affect the eyes, kidneys, peripheral nerves, etc.
  • Improve insulin-dependent tissue conditions

In Type 2 Diabetes, fetal stem cells can help:

  • Restore peripheral tissue sensitivity to insulin
  • Reduce atherogenic hyperinsulinemia
  • Decrease the production of glucose and pathologic lipids by liver cells
  • Improve the condition of arterial walls
  • Reduce the patient’s dose of medications

Diabetes Type I and Type II Fetal Stem Cell Treatment at EmCell

We offer fetal stem cell therapy for diabetes, which has been shown effective in both types of this disease.

Diabetes mellitus Type 2 develops when the pancreas can’t provide enough insulin or when the body can’t effectively use the insulin it produces.

Diabetes mellitus Type 1 develops when the immune system cells destroy the pancreas beta cells, so insulin secretion significantly decreases.

Our fetal stem cell treatment has been shown to help with both types.

About treatment

The main goal of fetal stem cell treatment at EmCell is to improve the patient’s management of disease. This can lead to potentially lowering the doses of medications as well as potentially preventing diabetes-related complications within commonly effected organs such as: eyes, blood vessels, nervous system and kidneys.

EmCell offers 2 possible treatment courses for Diabetes Mellitus:

  • 2-day fetal stem cell treatment, which is mainly for those patients who have their proper treatment regimen of medications, and there is no need for any adjustment in medication. 
  • 3-day fetal stem cell treatment which includes a complex therapy with the administration of both fetal stem cells and a proper medication treatment design.

Additional options can be added to our Diabetes treatment course at the patient’s request, such as: massages, hyperbaric oxygenation (HBO), facial treatment with fetal stem cells, and Halo therapy (salt room).

We have observed that after fetal stem cell treatment patients have demonstrated stable improvements in their condition: 65% with diabetes Type 1 and 74% with the diabetes Type 2. More often, in order to maintain such results, a patient needs to be willing to perform a change in lifestyle and to follow doctors’ recommendations.
