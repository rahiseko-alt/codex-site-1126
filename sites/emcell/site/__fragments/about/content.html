The origin of EmCell and stem cell therapies

1985
1985
A team of scientists, including the original founders of EmCell, commenced research within the field of fetal stem cells in animal studies. Animal studies showed great promise, prompting this team to prepare for the clinical application of fetal stem cells in humans.
1987
1987
The first phase of using fetal stem cell treatment in humans was by successfully designing a treatment for cytostatic myelodepression and aplastic anemia at the National Medical University of Ukraine.
1991
1991
Professor O. Smikodub, the original founder of the protocols used for the application of fetal stem cell treatment in humans, conducted the first fully registered fetal stem cell treatment at the hematologic department of the National Children’s Specialized Hospital, Okhmatdyt.
1994
1994
On February 2, the EmCell Clinic was founded in Kyiv, establishing the world’s first ever specialized center to use fetal stem cells for therapeutic purposes in humans. The science of obtaining, sorting, testing, and successfully placing fetal stem cells in EmCell’s own proprietary suspensions was established.
2005
2005
A BBC documentary called “Last Hope” covering EmCell’s work in fetal stem cells was released to the public garnering EmCell larger public attention in Europe.
2011
2011
Ukraine introduced new regulations for fetal stem cells by introducing a special license to be acquired to be legally allowed to study and apply fetal stem cells for clinical use. EmCell is the first in Ukraine to acquire this license, allowing for the world’s first ever fully licensed fetal stem cell bank.
2013
2013
After continued success treating various ailments utilizing EmCell’s fetal stem cell technology, EmCell relocates into its own building providing a single location for EmCell’s specialized biotechnology laboratory and treatment center.
2016
2016
A feature documentary focusing exclusively on fetal stem cells, entitled “The God Cells”, was released. A significant focus on EmCell’s laboratory and treatment process was documented in the movie, garnering EmCell larger attention in the Americas and throughout the world.
2019
2019
EmCell relocated into a brand new state-of-the art facility. EmCell’s new facility is the product of four years of research, planning, and construction. EmCell’s current facility is the world’s only fully functioning exclusive fetal stem cell laboratory and clinic of its kind.
Over the years, we have performed thousands of fetal stem cell treatments. Our cell therapy helped to prolong life and improve life quality to thousands of patients including those suffering from the incurable diseases who lost any hope for recovery.

EmCell today

Clinical Department

EmCell’s clinical department consists of highly qualified and experienced medical doctors with various specializations, and are the original authors of EmCell’s patented technology. Some of our medical doctors have been with us since our beginnings, holding additional scientific degrees such as PhDs.
Our clinical department is also supported by highly qualified nurses and assistants, while also serving as a diagnostic center including ultrasound, ECG, EchoCG, Doppler, Spirography, Densitometry, and more.

Laboratory Complex

Biotechnology Laboratory

The heart of the Laboratory Complex is a GMP compliance Biotechnological Laboratory, where the fetal stem cell preparations are created in accordance with unique original technology developed by our scientist’s team. We stay in touch with contemporary technologies and we use the most relevant techniques and modern equipment to ensure the highest quality and total safety of our products.

Laboratory Complex

Research & Development Department

Our scientific team assures quality, safety, and efficacy while verifying the full characteristics of each fetal stem cell preparation to fully maximize a positive outcome for each patient. We continually strive to improve our fetal stem cell preparations while exploring new effective protocols to increase the potential of our existing treatments. We are always seeking new horizons to compliment our existing ones.

Laboratory Complex

PCR Laboratory

Safety comes first. We use real-time PCR technology for identifying the RNA and DNA of multiple viral and bacterial agents such as HIV-1/2, HCV, HBV, CMV, HSV-1/2, EBV, HPV, COVID-19, Treponema palladium, Chlamidium trachomatis, Mycoplasma genitalis, Ureaplasma Urealyticum, and more. We have multiple layers of testing technology to insure each and every application is safe for out patients.

Laboratory Complex

Diagnostic Laboratory

EmCell’s well-trained specialists in our own ISO 15189 accredited laboratory perform our patient’s lab tests that give us the necessary information about each patient’s health status to allow EmCell to design the most personalized therapy for each patient. This includes immunophenotyping, biochemical investigation, cytology, hormone status, and more.

Laboratory Complex

The Cryobank

Our modern cryostorage facility maintains more than 20,000 high quality living proliferative fetal stem cell preparations using EmCell’s proprietary method of programmed “suspension”. These suspensions are classified by type, allowing for a diversified array of fetal stem cells giving us the opportunity to personalize our therapy to each patient’s specific needs.
We keep up to date with modern technology and innovations and advance our methods of work in order to ensure the highest quality and safety standards of the cells.
Our specialists in all fields continuously work on the advancement of stem cell suspension preparation and proper application technologies. They write experience-based articles on different aspects of stem cell application, learn and introduce new methods and approaches.
The best reward for EmCell is successful treatment and improvements in our patients, their words of gratitude and smiles on their faces.
