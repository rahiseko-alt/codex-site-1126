Danya Safonkin’s Journey and New Possibilities in ASD Treatment

The Beginning: When the World Suddenly Changes

Danya is a boy from Ukraine who was diagnosed at the age of 4 with autism spectrum disorder (ASD), cognitive epileptiform disintegration, and developmental delay.

His early development was typical. However, at the age of 1.5, after vaccination, serious changes began:

•speech regression

•communication difficulties

•changes in facial expression

•neurological symptoms

Doctors had no answers. Only hard words were heard: “Accept it. This is profound intellectual disability. Nothing can be done.” 

But his mother refused to accept that.

Years of Struggle and Search

The family went through everything:

•consultations with numerous doctors

•various therapy approaches

•continuous work with special education professionals

Danya’s mother, a trained educator, became his strongest support. She even accompanied him at school, believing in the power of inclusion and socialization.

And there were results—gradual, but meaningful:

•development of reading and writing skills

•partial social adaptation

Then the war changed everything. Relocation, loss of school routine, isolation — and regression returned. A new solution was needed.

When Hope Appears

After years of searching, the family discovered EmCell.

At the time of admission, Danya had:

•a vocabulary of about 20 words

•limited communication skills

•difficulties with writing

•lack of eye contact

•stereotypical movements

•poor coordination

•limited self-care skills

Treatment: A Comprehensive Approach

Danya underwent a course of therapy that included:

•intravenous administration of fetal stem cells

•intranasal and inhalation delivery

•hyperbaric oxygen therapy

•a 3-month home exosome therapy

Before and after treatment, MRI tractography was performed.

The results showed improved connectivity between key language centers of the brain — Broca’s and Wernicke’s areas.

But the most important changes cannot be measured by scans alone.

Results: When a Child Begins to Open Up to the World

After therapy, Danya began to change. What emerged was the foundation of development—awareness and connection with the world.

In everyday life:

•became more independent (hygiene, daily routines)

•followed instructions without repetition

•improved coordination

•started making his own decisions

In communication:

•began asking questions

•maintained eye contact with the camera

•expressed thoughts and desires

In emotional development:

•shows emotions

•demonstrates self-awareness

•wants to look like his peers

And one moment stood out above all.

Danya independently wrote:

“Dogs can smell a person’s loneliness — especially mine…” This is more than a sentence. It is evidence of deep thinking and an inner world that is beginning to unfold.

Expert Perspective

Danya’s special education teacher reports:

•improved attention

•speech development

•better self-control

•increased awareness of time

•improved coordination

•faster skill acquisition

These are comprehensive changes that indicate a significant developmental breakthrough.

What Lies Behind This Story

This is not only a story about medicine.

It is a story about:

•a mother’s belief

•daily dedication

•refusing to accept limitations

•searching for possibilities where others see none

And about the fact that even in the most challenging cases, new opportunities can emerge.

Conclusion

Danya’s results are not a coincidence.

They are the result of a combination of:

•an innovative treatment approach

•consistent rehabilitation

•unwavering family support

What once seemed impossible has become reality.

If your child faces similar challenges, it is important to know: options exist.

Danya’s story continues.

And there is much more ahead.
