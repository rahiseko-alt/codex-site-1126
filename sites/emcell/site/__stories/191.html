Health update three month following the treatment at EmCell
Patient: S.E.
Gender: Male
Age: 6 y.o.
Country: Macedonia
Diagnosis: Genetically confirmed spinal muscular atrophy type II, chronic form, relative stabilization stage, muscle tetraparesis, hereditary proximal spinal muscular atrophy.

Dear doctors and the entire EmCell team,

It has now been three months since S.’s FSC treatment, and he is doing well. He happily attends his swimming sessions and speech and language therapy. These specialists were the first to notice improvements in his swallowing and in the function of his chest muscles.

Nine months after treatment, S.’s condition remains stable. We enjoy taking him on walks outdoors, and he has been enthusiastic about trying the new exercises recommended for his back. Our orthopedist recently noticed an increase in the angle of his spinal curvature, so we are paying close attention to this as part of his ongoing care.

We are considering returning to EmCell at the end of August 2027 in hopes of slowing the progression of the complications associated with his condition for as long as possible.

Thank you to the entire EmCell team for your care and support.
