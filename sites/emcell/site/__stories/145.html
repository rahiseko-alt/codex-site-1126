Report on K.K.B. 4 months after treatment
Patient: K. K. B.
Gender: Male
Age: 8 y.o.
Country: Great Britain
Diagnosis: Autism

Hi, 

Since our last visit to EmCell we have seen subtle changes in cognition. His school and speech and language therapist have noticed too. 

Over the past month we have noticed that K. has gained a greater ability to climb whilst visits to park. He is faster and his gross motor skills seems to have improved greatly. He can climb and put his foot and arms in the right place and navigate various obstacles. He is also spending longer in the park before he gets tired,  he used to stay approximately an hour but now stays longer. He also now is understanding how to use bumper cars which is due to his greater understanding. 

Hope this is helpful.
