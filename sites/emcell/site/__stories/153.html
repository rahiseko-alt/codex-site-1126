Health update of the patient S.A.A. 10 months after the 4th treatment
Patient: S.A.A.
Gender: Female
Age: 19 y.o.
Country: Great Britain
Diagnosis: Autism

Hello again, 

I want to say that having been through one of the most difficult times we ever had with S., we have a different person emerging, more like a young lady.

She is extremely clean now, sleeping great, no verbal noises or tics at all, no vandalism, basically no bad behaviors whatsoever. 

Most exciting of all is that if I help her with the first syllable or two, she is speaking all day about what she's doing and feeling in sentences. For some reason I need to start the sentence for her then she finishes it off, fluently and with ease.

This is amazing and she is truly excited about it. 

She has also started running again so I take her - just 2km every 3 days but she's enjoying it a lot!

She is smiling and happy and I see a young lady as if by magic before me.

Thank you.
