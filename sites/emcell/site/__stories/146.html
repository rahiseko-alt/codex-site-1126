Health update of A.R.S. 3 weeks after treatment
Patient: A. R. S.
Gender: Male
Age: 6 y.o.
Country: the Netherlands, UAE
Diagnosis: Autism

It has been three and a half weeks since his first transfusion, and I am stunned by the remarkable improvement in his energy levels - namely endurance. He was always quick to fatigue, and now his energy levels are en par with his peers. It is an absolute joy to see him able to keep up with other children and stay enthusiastic throughout an activity from start to finish. 

Another remarkable improvement that we have seen are his speech skills. It is an improvement that we have never seen in this short span of time. 

We are currently able to have a back-and-forth conversation with A. for several minutes. His sentences have multiplied, and he is using new words. We are finally getting to “know” our son; his likes, his dislikes and his interests in details we have never known before. Prior to this amazing change, we primarily relied on our intuition to guide us. My husband and I are utterly delighted - for ourselves and for him. This improvement promises to open up opportunities for him to make friends and participate with more ease in school.

A. still has speech challenges - but he continues to work with a speech therapist to help him overcome them as well. Given that it has only been three weeks since his stem cell treatment, my husband and I feel hopeful to continue to see growth and development in this his speech and language skills, and perhaps many other areas too. Fingers crossed. 

Thank you for following up. I will continue to update you as time passes. We continue to challenge him daily, to maximise the potential of his cells.
