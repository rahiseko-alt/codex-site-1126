Patient: Ms. J.G.
Gender: Female
Age: 45 y.o.
Country: USA
Diagnosis: Compound myopic astigmatism in both eyes. Stage III keratoconus of the left eye. Corneal cross-linking of the left eye.

Dear Valentina,

Thanks for contacting me.

As you know, my main diagnosis was stage 3 keratoconus, and I was planning to undergo corneal cross-linking after my treatment at EmCell. I viewed the stem cell treatment as preparation for the procedure. Of course, no one at the clinic made any guarantees about the outcome.

After receiving fetal stem cell treatment at EmCell, the condition of my cornea improved, including an increase in its central thickness. This allowed me to successfully undergo corneal cross-linking on my left eye.

Over the past year, my vision has remained stable. According to corneal topography, my corneal thickness has remained unchanged, while my corneal astigmatism has decreased by 2 diopters according to refraction testing. These results indicate that my visual function has remained stable.

I continue to be regularly monitored by my ophthalmologist and will keep you informed about my progress.

Thank you to the entire EmCell team.
