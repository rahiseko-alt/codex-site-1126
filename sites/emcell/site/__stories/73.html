Improvements in condition of P.P. suffering from DMD
Patient: P.P.
Gender: Male
Age: 25 y.o.
Country: USA
Diagnosis: Duchenne’s Muscular Dystrophy

I’d like to tell you that I’m feeling better now. After treatment, I noticed more power in my arms and legs. The size of my shoulder and hip muscles has increased. I feel no pain in my back.
