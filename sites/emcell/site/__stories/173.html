Health update 5 months following the therapy
Patient: T. K.
Gender: Male
Age: 7 y.o.
Country: Serbia
Diagnosis: Autism

Before EmCell therapy: the boy had cognitive deficit, decreased ability to concentrate, problems with communication, socialization difficulties as well as language deficit – the boy’s vocabulary included about 20 words, which he could use consciously.

Five months following EmCell therapy:  the boy starting speaking in full sentences for the first time in his life; he started to sit at the table during meals with his family, for the first time ever, eating roasted bacon with eggs, also for the first time ever; he started singing songs; his vocabulary extrended. What a progress!
