Feedback of the patient S.M.A. after his 2nd stem cell treatment
Patient: S.M.A.
Gender: Male
Age: 34 y.o.
Country: Hungary
Diagnosis: Partial optic nerve atrophy in both eyes. Compound myopic astigmatism in both eyes.

Dear EmCell Doctors,

After my first treatment at EmCell, I gradually began to notice improvements in my distance vision. I also found that I was adapting better in low-light and evening conditions.

My current prescription glasses work well for me, but I had to reduce the strength of my contact lenses because they had become too strong for me.

After returning to EmCell for my second course of treatment, I noticed that my vision remained stable and that my peripheral vision had improved. My computerized visual field test showed an expansion of my field of vision and improved sensitivity to light.

My near vision remains very good, which I am especially happy about.

I’m looking forward to my next visit.
