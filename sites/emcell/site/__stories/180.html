A Family’s feedback: One Year of Stability and Meaningful Changes After Treatment
Patient: Mr. I.W.
Gender: Male
Age: 74 y.o.
Country: Indonesia
Diagnosis: Alzheimer’s disease. Coronary artery disease. Diffuse cardiosclerosis. Essential arterial hypertension, grade II, stage 3, risk 4. Heart failure, stage IIA, without reduced left ventricular ejection fraction.

Here is what the family of a patient shared with us following his treatment at EmCell:

When we came to EmCell, my father’s condition had changed dramatically over the course of his illness. He was completely bedridden and didn't recognize the members of his family well. His speech was limited and he spent most of his time in bed.

This was especially difficult for us because he had once been much more independent. At home, he would sometimes wander around the apartment without understanding where he was. On one occasion, he even went outside and became lost in the courtyard of our building. It was heartbreaking for our family to see him in such a condition.

Two months after treatment

During the first couple of months, we did not see the deterioration we had been afraid of. Instead, his condition appeared to have stabilized.

What surprised us most was that he began responding to some of our questions. His answers were still very limited, and he remained disoriented, but even these small changes were very meaningful to our family.

Four months after treatment

By the fourth month, we noticed that he had become more active. He was able to sit up on his own and began answering some of our questions.

Although he was still disoriented, he became more involved in simple everyday interactions. One thing that particularly stood out to us was that he was able to express what he wanted to eat. These may seem like small things, but for us, they made a real difference.

Twelve months after treatment

It has now been a year since his treatment, and his condition remains stable. The changes we noticed during the first few months have been maintained.

We are very grateful that his condition has not continued to deteriorate in the way we had experienced before treatment. For our family, having this stability and seeing these small but meaningful changes has meant a great deal.

Thank you, EmCell, for giving our family hope and for helping us have more meaningful moments with our father.
