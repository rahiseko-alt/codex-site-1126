Health update on Mr. A.T.: after the 2nd treatment
Patient: Mr. A.T.
Gender: Male
Age: 67 y.o.
Country: Great Britain
Diagnosis: Alzheimer's Disease

Here is what Mr. A.T.’s wife shared with us following his second treatment at EmCell:

Before my husband came to EmCell, I felt like I was slowly losing the person I had known for so many years. His memory was getting worse almost every day. He would ask me the same question over and over again without realizing he had already asked it, and sometimes he simply couldn’t find the right words in the middle of a conversation.

During the last couple of months before his treatment, things became especially difficult. He would confuse dates, lose his keys or phone, and sometimes become very confused about what was happening around him. At one point, he even accused me and our son of stealing from him. It was very painful for all of us, and I could see how frightened and confused he was.

About five months after he started treatment at EmCell, both our family and his psychiatrist began to notice a real change. Most importantly, his condition had stopped getting worse. The suspicious thoughts about being robbed gradually disappeared, and his speech became much clearer. He was more able to communicate and seemed more like himself again.

A year later, when we returned to EmCell, the improvements he had gained were still stable. That was very important to our family because we could see that the changes had lasted.

I am incredibly grateful to EmCell for helping my husband regain parts of himself that I was afraid we had lost for good. Seeing him more like the person he used to be has meant so much to our whole family. We have already planned his next course of treatment with EmCell.
