A Wife’s Story: Seeing Her Husband Return to Himself
Patient: Mr. T.B.
Gender: Male
Age: 60 y.o.
Country: USA
Diagnosis: Alzheimer's disease; essential arterial hypertension stage II, grade 2, risk 3; heart failure stage I.

When my husband first came to EmCell, he was very different from the person he had always been. He had worked as an accountant all his life and had always managed our family finances, but he could no longer do these things. He became irritable, suspicious, and sometimes aggressive. He would hide things in unusual places — once, we found his keys in the freezer — and he even accused me of spying on him.

About three months after treatment, we noticed that his condition had stabilized, and his suspicious thoughts began to ease. By eight months, they had disappeared completely. He became sociable again, was in better spirits, and became much more involved in our family life.

When we returned to EmCell eighteen months later, these improvements were still there. I am deeply grateful to EmCell for helping my husband feel like himself again and giving our family hope.
