Feedback of the patient with chronic obstructive pulmonary disease after stem cell therapy
Patient: M.B.
Gender: Male
Age: 60 y.o.
Country: Serbia
Diagnosis: Stage III, grade 2-3 chronic obstructive pulmonary disease. Lung emphysema. Diffuse pneumosclerosis. Grade II-III respiratory failure. IHD: stable effort angina, NYHA Class I-II. Grade I heart failure with preserved left ventricular systolic function, NYHA Class II.

Dear friends from EmCell,

A month after treatment I feel the following changes:

  • easier to move around and I have a lot more energy
  • weather change that happens every day in Serbia (rain and sun) is now easier to withstand. I used call my doctor to give me the injection of aminophylline after each weather change, now there is no need for this
  • I feel like something is happening in my lungs but do not know to explain what
  • it is much easier to breathe, and as I take the breath to the fullest, as if I can take a deeper breath

I did spirometry and the results are the same as before the stem cell, but I feel much better.
