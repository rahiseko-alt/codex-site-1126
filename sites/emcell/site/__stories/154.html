Health update 9 months after the 4th treatment
Patient: B.Z.
Gender: Male
Age: 14 y.o.
Country: Slovenia
Diagnosis: Autism spectrum disorder

Dear EmCell team,

B. is doing great. He has made progress in all areas, and even at school, the teachers praise him. For communication, he uses two picture cards (for drink – a glass, and an apple). He understands all words and can point to what he wants – something we couldn’t have imagined some time ago.

His sleep has also improved. He now sleeps through the night from 9 PM to 6 AM, whereas in the past, he would wake up at 2 or 3 AM and couldn’t fall back asleep. He has no health issues.

At school, he attends swimming therapy, music therapy, helps in the school kitchen, goes to physiotherapy, fitness, and walks or cycles a lot. We also go to shops, have coffee, go to the pharmacy, and more.

You have truly improved our quality of life – both B.s and the whole family’s. 

Thank you.

We hope to see you again soon.
