Patient S.M. with ALS feels better after stem cell treatment
Patient: S.M.
Gender: Male
Age: 68 y.o.
Country: Cyprus
Diagnosis: Amyotrophic lateral sclerosis. Bulbar palsy. Spastic tetraparesis.

My dad is doing fine, he has put on some weight since stem cell transplants, overall looks very healthy, his colour is good as well. So in general i would say he is in good condition!
