I assess my general health as improved. Following my treatments at EMCELL in December 2025, I feel that my physical recovery is on positive trajectory, especially compared to the period before my catheter ablation in February 2025.

I find that my energy levels are becoming much more consistent. I no longer feel the same fluctuations, likely because my body is recovering more effectively from my various treatments.

I have observed several positive changes, specifically an improvement in my skin quality and a noticeable increase in the mobility of my arm. I am encouraged by these visible signs of progress.

 Observations & Improvements

1. Physical Rejuvenation & Appearance

  • Hair Quality: My hair feels significantly stronger and healthier, much like it did when I was younger.

  • HbA1c Improvement: My HbA1c has decreased to 6.0% (as of March 14, 2026), compared to 6.2% in May 2025. (Before it was 6.9%)

4. Emotional & Psychological State

  • Calmness over Power: I do not experience a "high-energy boost." Instead, I feel a profound, stable sense of calm.
  • Reduced Anxiety: My nervousness has subsided, and I feel significantly less daily pressure.

5. Sleep Quality

  • I am achieving a deeper, more consistent sleep without the usual interruptions.

6. Respiratory & Vocal Health

  • Singing: I have noticed a marked improvement in my singing ability.

7. Physical Mobility & Old Injuries

  • Range of Motion: I can now raise my arm completely straight—a significant improvement in mobility.

8. Vision

  • My eyesight feels sharper and more reliable.

Clinical Summary

  • Successes: Triglycerides and HDL (Good Cholesterol) have both returned to normal ranges since May 2025. HbA1c has also improved and is now within the target range at 6.0%.
  • Ongoing Monitoring: Fasting Glucose has improved from 141 to 127 mg/dL.
