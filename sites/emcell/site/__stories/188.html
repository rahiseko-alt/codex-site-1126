Improvements in condition of Mrs. L.G. suffering from RA
Patient: Mrs. L.G.
Gender: Female
Age: 42 y.o.
Country: Romania
Diagnosis: Rheumatoid arthritis

One month after treatment

It’s been a month now. I haven’t noticed any significant changes yet, but getting up in the morning has become a little easier. My morning stiffness now goes away in about 40–50 minutes, compared with an hour and a half before.

My joints are still sensitive to changes in the weather, so for now I haven’t reduced my usual dose of nimesulide. My doctor says this is normal and that the treatment may take some time to fully take effect.

Three months after treatment

I’m starting to notice a real difference now. The swelling in my hands has almost completely gone down, and I can comfortably hold a cup and button my clothes again.

I also had my blood work done. My ESR decreased from 38 to 20, and my rheumatoid factor has also dropped significantly. My rheumatologist said I could reduce my methotrexate dose slightly.

Six months after treatment

It has now been six months since the procedure, and my doctor confirms that I am in stable remission. I no longer have any swelling, and my joints are no longer showing signs of inflammation.

I have completely stopped taking the painkillers I used to take so frequently. I’m now only taking my maintenance dose of methotrexate as part of my regular treatment.

I feel like I’ve finally returned to a normal life — I’ve even started taking yoga classes again!
