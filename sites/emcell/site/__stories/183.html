Feedback of the patient Mr. S.T. after stem cell treatment
Patient: Mr. S.T.
Gender: Male
Age: 48 y.o.
Country: Denmark
Diagnosis: Intestinal adenocarcinoma, status post surgery and chemotherapy

Therapy I received at EmCell helped me get back to a more active and fulfilling life.

I began noticing changes in my condition quite soon after the treatment. I felt less weak and gradually started to regain my energy.

A few months later, I also noticed a significant improvement in my digestion. The abdominal pain and discomfort that had been bothering me were gone. I was able to eat a wider variety of foods and, for the first time in a while, started gaining weight again.

My blood tests improved as well. My anemia and low white blood cell count were no longer present.

I also felt much better emotionally. I had more energy, a better mood, and, most importantly, I started feeling like a healthy person again.

I am very grateful to the entire EmCell team for their care and support.

S.T.
