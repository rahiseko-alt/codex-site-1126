Patient: Mr. B.A.
Gender: Male
Age: 51 y.o.
Country: Spain
Diagnosis: Lung cancer, status post surgery, radiation therapy, and chemotherapy.

Dear EmCell Team,

Thank you for checking in on me.

Here is my update: I started noticing improvements soon after my stem cell treatment. Within the first month, my general weakness decreased, and I started sleeping better.

By three months, I was able to return to work, and I was extremely happy about that. At that point, I was working about three to four hours a day. My memory and concentration had also improved, and I started to feel like I was getting back on track.

Six months after treatment, I had a follow-up appointment with my oncologist, and my test results were much better. For the first time in many years, my hemoglobin was within the normal range. My immune system, liver, and kidney test results had also improved, and I was feeling much better overall.

That gave me a lot of hope and motivation. I’m very happy to be in remission now and grateful for how far I’ve come.

I’m planning to return to EmCell in the future for another treatment.

Thank you to the entire EmCell team for your care and support.
