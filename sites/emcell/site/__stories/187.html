A Patient’s experience with Age-Related Macular Degeneration
Patient: Mr. J.P.M.
Gender: Male
Age: 69 y.o.
Country: USA
Diagnosis: Age-related macular degeneration (dry form) in both eyes. Early-stage cataracts in both eyes. Low-grade hyperopia in both eyes.

Dear EmCell

I would like to sincerely thank the entire EmCell team, and especially my treating physician, for the care I received during my treatment for macular degeneration.

The diagnosis was frightening, but thanks to the high level of care I received, we achieved a very encouraging result. Within just two to three months after treatment, the distortion in my vision had significantly decreased, and in some situations, I could barely notice it anymore. The gray spot in my field of vision had also partially disappeared.

I recently had a comprehensive examination with my local retinal specialist, and he was encouraged by the results of my retinal OCT.

I am very grateful to the entire EmCell team and would recommend the center to anyone facing a similar problem.

With my sincere thanks,
