Feedback by a patient R.P.M. with diabetes after stem cell treatment
Patient: R.P.M.
Gender: Male
Age: 59 y.o.
Country: Great Britain
Diagnosis: Moderate, poorly managed type 2 diabetes mellitus. Diabetic peripheral polyneuropathy. Diabetic universal (macro- and micro-) angiopathy. Diabetic hepatosis. Stage II grade 2 essential hypertension without cardiac failure, risk group IV. Grade III obesity. Benign prostate hyperplasia. Mixed type erectile dysfunction: endocrine, vascular and diencephalic.

My condition has improved and my weight has been steadily reducing. I have more energy and am able to do things which I haven't done for many years. I can walk much better and faster and my peripheral neuropathy has improved a lot.

Clarity of mind and spiritual calm is better, however there is no improvement for my erectile dysfunction but overall my energy and zest for life is greatly improved, my blood sugar is also definitely better and under better control, usually between 6-8. My physical strength and immune system is also much better and can resist the damp English weather.
