One month after treatment

Hi Valentina. We're already back home. The trip wasn't easy, but I felt energetic and strong. My sleep has also improved, so I'm hopeful that I will continue to see more improvements in the future.

I’m continuing my regular immunosuppressive treatment along with a low dose of prednisone. I’ll keep you updated on any changes in my health.

Three months after treatment

Hello! I’m feeling quite well. My morning stiffness now lasts no more than about 30 minutes, and I am not currently experiencing any signs of inflammation in my joints.

I continue to take my immunosuppressive medication. After discussing it with my family doctor, we may gradually reduce my corticosteroid dose since I am not experiencing any signs of inflammation.

Six months after treatment

Hello, and thank you for continuing to check in on me and stay in touch with your patients after treatment. I’m feeling well.

Under my doctor’s supervision, I was able to stop taking prednisone because there are no signs of inflammation in my joints. I have also noticed some improvement in my joint mobility, and I find it easier to climb stairs.

My latest blood tests showed an ESR of 10 mm/h and a CRP level of 4, both within the normal range. My DAS28 score has also decreased from 3.1 to 2.6.

I’m very pleased with the progress I’ve made so far and will continue to follow my doctor’s recommendations.
