Feedback from the parents of patient with SMA
Patient: A.O.B.
Gender: Female
Age: 6 y.o.
Country: UAE
Diagnosis: Spinal muscular atrophy, type I. Severe proximal hereditary motor neuropathy. Dysphagia. Grade I thoracic kyphoscoliosis.

Dear doctors and the entire EmCell team,

We are happy to share that O. has shown positive changes following her treatment at EmCell.

About six months after treatment, we began noticing better physical endurance during her rehabilitation sessions. She can now swim about 10–15 meters in the pool with assistive equipment while actively trying to use her arms and legs. She is also able to maintain head control for longer periods, up to 15–20 minutes. We have also noticed that she has fewer episodes of choking when drinking liquids.

Between nine and twelve months after treatment, her episodes of constipation also became less frequent, and we no longer needed to use additional medication to help stimulate bowel movements.

Our family is currently on a waiting list for government-funded genetic therapy, and in the meantime, we are planning to continue treatment at EmCell to support the progress she has made for as long as possible.

We hope the war in Ukraine will end soon, and we hope to return for her next treatment in July or August 2027.

God bless you all.
